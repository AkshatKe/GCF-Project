👥 About CivicByte

💡 Our Purpose

CivicByte is a Civic Issue Reporting & Resolution System built to bridge the communication gap between citizens and local authorities. Our platform provides a simple, transparent, and efficient way to report civic issues — from potholes to broken streetlights.

🚀 Our Mission

To create a transparent ecosystem where every civic issue is logged, tracked, and resolved fast.

👁️ Our Vision

To be the leading platform for community-driven city maintenance.
